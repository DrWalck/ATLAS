Key equations: relative motion of particles

Translating frame (any coordinates)

B/A means "B as seen from A". The frame attached to A translates (does not rotate), so these hold in Cartesian, n–t or polar components alike.

Cartesian

Write each absolute velocity as x–y components, subtract. Best when headings are given as compass directions or angles from fixed axes.

Normal–tangential

Absolute velocities are along each path's tangent; accelerations have a_t = v̇ and a_n = v²/ρ. Resolve into one common set of axes before subtracting.

Polar

When the observer sits at a pivot and watches a point whose r and θ change: v = ṙ ê_r + rθ̇ ê_θ, a = (r̈ − rθ̇²) ê_r + (rθ̈ + 2ṙθ̇) ê_θ.

Rotating frame (observer on a turning platform)

Only when the frame itself rotates. The 2Ω × v_rel term is the Coriolis acceleration.
