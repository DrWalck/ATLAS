Key equations: work & energy in every frame

Principle of work & energy

Scalar equation — best when you want speed between two positions. Normal forces, tension in a fixed-length cord, and friction-free constraint forces do no work.

Work of common forces

Friction: Uf = −μkN·s (always negative). Gravity far from Earth: Vg = −GMm/r.

Conservation of energy

Vg = mgy near Earth, Ve = ½ks². Escape speed: T + Vg = 0 at infinity ⇒ vesc = √(2GM/R).

Cartesian (x–y)

Collars on rods, sliders with springs: measure Δy and the spring stretch directly from geometry.

Normal–tangential (n–t)

Energy gives the speed at a point on a curve; then ΣFn = mv²/ρ gives the normal force or the minimum speed to stay on a loop (N = 0).

Polar (r–θ)

Central forces (gravity, a spring to a fixed point) do work only through Δr. Pair energy with angular-momentum conservation to find rmin, rmax of an orbit.
