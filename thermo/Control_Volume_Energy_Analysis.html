Key equations: mass and energy rate balances for control volumes

Mass rate balance

AV is the volumetric flow rate (m³/s, ft³/min, gal/h…); divide by v (or multiply by ρ) to get mass flow. At steady state Σṁ_in = Σṁ_out.

Energy rate balance

Enthalpy h = u + pv already contains the flow work. Q̇ into the CV positive, Ẇ by the CV positive (shaft, electrical). Steady state: dE/dt = 0.

Steady state, one inlet / one exit

Cross out what the device does not do — that is the whole art of Chapter 4.

The standard devices

Nozzle / diffuser: Ẇ = 0, Q̇ ≈ 0 → h₁ + V₁²/2 = h₂ + V₂²/2. Turbine: Ẇ_cv = ṁ(h₁ − h₂) (+ KE if given), Q̇ ≈ 0. Compressor / pump: same, Ẇ_cv < 0 (work in). Throttling valve: h₂ = h₁. Heat exchanger: Ẇ = 0, Q̇ = 0 for the whole box → Σṁh in = Σṁh out. Mixing chamber: ṁ₁h₁ + ṁ₂h₂ = ṁ₃h₃.

Unit conversions that bite

Kinetic energy V²/2 comes out in m²/s² or ft²/s² — convert before adding it to h. In English units V²/2 needs g_c = 32.174 lbm·ft/lbf·s² and 778.17 ft·lbf/Btu: 32.174 × 778.17 = 25,037.
