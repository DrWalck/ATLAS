ISB axes

X anterior–posterior (points forward). Frontal-plane movements rotate about X. Y longitudinal / superior–inferior (points up, along the bone). Transverse-plane movements rotate about Y. Z medial–lateral (points to the subject's right). Sagittal-plane movements rotate about Z. Each segment carries its own local frame with the same letters at its centre of mass; the global frame sits at the pelvis origin (lower abdomen, between the ASIS). Movements are named for the plane they happen in, and rotate about the axis perpendicular to that plane.

Joint types

ball-and-socket (3 DOF) · condyloid / ellipsoid (2) · saddle (2) · hinge (1) · pivot (1) · plane / gliding (small slides) · modified hinge (1 + a little). Click any joint to see which it is.
